GEOMETRY AND PROCESSING
The intended design repeats a walk and moving 180-degree left turn after rotating their placement by pi. Thus BOTH walking displacement and moving-turn displacement reverse and cancel. The four main reference displacements are [+0.803018,-0.120820], [+0.499619,+0.397469], [-0.803018,+0.120820], [-0.499619,-0.397469] m. Right-foot pose-alignment bridges introduce a residual [-0.106166,+0.017100] m (0.107534 m); no terminal closure solver or forced endpoint is applied. Geometry.json breaks out all bridge and segment displacement.

Both walk segments reuse newly generated walk_2010, crop source frames 0–60 inclusive (0–3.0 s), speed 1.0. Both turns reuse newly generated turn_2014, frames 0–47 inclusive (0–2.35 s), speed 0.7, resampled to 3.34 s. 20 Hz GMR is resampled to 50 Hz. Each segment has 0.3 s endpoint easing and an initial heading normalization. A smooth cubic heading offset corrects the selected walk crop by -15.797035 deg to net zero and turn by +0.986036 deg to net +180. It rotates pose headings AND root displacement increments; no reflection or left/right swap. These are explicit reference calibrations, not untouched generator output. A quintic bridge chooses the lower outgoing foot (right in all three cases), aligns the incoming pose to that foot, and solves leg/root IK to keep its world position; the other foot has up to 0.025 m clearance. The last reference pose is held 2 s. See composition.json for all coordinates and processing.

Training turn command -pi means LEFT; G1 world +yaw (CCW, local +X forward/+Y left/+Z up) means LEFT. Full human turn FK has +185.479 deg and original GMR +178.539 deg. Actual turns have clear net left rotation; they are not perfectly monotone: brief preparatory clockwise excursions reach -7.543 and -8.727 deg relative to each turn start, followed by the large left turns. Total negative yaw variation is ~13.2 deg each. This is recorded in additional_checks.json, not concealed by taking absolute yaw.
