Status: research and implementation; no improvement claimed yet.

Scope: simulation-only online PPO for one reference-conditioned G1 policy across
20 official FrankenMotion motion classes. No historical specialist weights,
action labels, teacher rollouts, per-task policy routing or policy distillation.
The existing shared20 policy may be evaluated as a baseline only. Primary new
policy starts randomly; optional official pretrained initialization must have
verified public provenance and be reported as a separate experiment.

Research decisions:
- DeepMimic: imitation can be an RL objective from kinematic references, without
  expert action labels. Reference state initialization reduces exploration cost.
- BeyondMimic: use the published tracking MDP and fixed reward recipe as a
  reproducible baseline. Exclude its second-stage policy distillation.
- SONIC: a generalist pretrained baseline, not a feasible from-scratch scaling
  target on one GPU. No claim to reproduce its dataset or compute scale.
- mjlab 1.1.1 is already installed with MuJoCo/Warp 3.5.0, rsl-rl 4.0.1,
  torch 2.7.0, warp 1.12.0. Pin these, record source hashes and local changes.

Pre-training engineering audit:
- The mjlab G1 foot collision shapes are capsules. Floor alignment uses actual
  capsule endpoints and radius, once per clip. No per-frame floor projection.
- Reference velocity is measured at the body frame origin (mjOBJ_XBODY), not
  inertial COM (mjOBJ_BODY). A finite-difference test detects the difference.
- Explicit environment reset does not refresh upstream relative tracking targets.
  Added TrackingEnvironment.reset() to refresh after FK without advancing time.
  Discarded initial baseline_current run and its first-step-failure evaluation.
- Six real-environment tests passed (tests_v3.log). Reset body error tolerance is
  5mm because upstream RSI clamps joint angles to soft limits; actual max 2.25mm.
- Train/validation reference audit covers 120 clips. Zero clips have more than
  10% of frames with foot penetration exceeding 2cm. This is not a semantic or
  dynamic feasibility guarantee; no clips were filtered based on this audit.
- Tracking-success thresholds fixed before valid trained-policy evaluation:
  complete, mean global anchor error<0.15m, mean global tracked-body error<0.20m,
  mean per-frame joint RMS error<0.40rad. Completion alone is separately reported.
- Primary failure-inclusive errors append fixed errors for the unobserved tail:
  0.5m root/body, 1rad joints. Also report observed-horizon errors and failure time.
- Formal runs: baseline_current_v2 then preview_future_v2; 400 environments,
  24 PPO steps/update, 3000 updates each. Validation at 500/1000/1500/2000/2500/2999.
  Preview offsets 5/10/20 at 50Hz (0.1/0.2/0.4s); end-of-clip clamped.
