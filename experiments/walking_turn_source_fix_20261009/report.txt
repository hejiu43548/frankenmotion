Walking-turn source repair — 2026-10-09

Problem and change
The initial Hydra integration retained main's legacy turn endpoint definition (zero requested speed and positive0.45–1.5rad) instead of the previously agreed walking-turn data. This repair reconnects the reviewed walking_turn_v2 admission and regenerated data_v2 caches to the new20-task training entry. No standalone turn/spin trainer or checkpoint is imported. Model module topology and parameter counts are unchanged.
